Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in for a full refund. Within 48 hours, 50% of the amount is refunded.

Do you offer group or custom packages?

Yes - share your destination, dates and number of travellers in the form above and we will send a custom quote within 24 hours.

Which payment methods are accepted?

UPI, credit/debit cards and net banking, or choose Pay at Hotel for selected stays.

Is airport pickup available?

Select hotels include airport pickup. Look for the “Airport Pickup” amenity on the hotel page or use the amenity filter.
