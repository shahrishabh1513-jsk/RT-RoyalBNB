Who we are

Royal stays, made simple & honest

RT Royal BnB is a hotel management & booking platform from Vadodara, Gujarat. We bring together handpicked heritage hotels, havelis, desert camps and sea-view resorts across Udaipur, Jaisalmer, Jaipur, Jodhpur and Dwarka — with clear prices, real distances to the sights and people who actually pick up the phone.
